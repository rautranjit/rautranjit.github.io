In simple words, backlinks are links on other sites that point to your site, essentially pointing to your content. We can say that backlink is a process by which a link is formed between two websites. It is a record of past activity between two web pages. It is an official record in the database of the search engines that categorizes the links, between the two web pages. A backlink is a physical link, which a person can see. Backlinks and search engine optimization Backlinks and backlink development is an important process for search engine optimization. SEOs find backlinks helpful for ranking a website in search engines. Therefore a backlink is one of the most important and important types of web pages for SEO.Backlinks come in two types: do-follow and no-follow backlinks. So, what they are. Let’s learnA Do-follow backlink is a type of link that the search engine recognizes as having a lot of quality and value. A Do-follow backlink will give you an active score in the search engine. There are two ways of making do-follow backlinks. One way is to find a site and contact the webmaster to request a link. Another way is to find a site with a similar niche and contact the webmaster to request to add your link to their site. A bonus way is that you should check the broken link of the site on which you want to make a backlink and then contact the site owner to put your link on their site.A No-follow backlink is a link that does not pass on the link juice to the external page. Link juice is the term widely used in the Search Engine Optimization (SEO) universe that simply tells whether the value is passed or not from a site to another. It means if a site has a no-follow backlink refer to an external site, then while crawling the particular site any search engine bots will ignore it assuming the preferred site isn’t valuable or worthy content. As a result, the external website does not get the juice from the no-follow backlink. Usually, This link is used to allow users to share links without passing on the link juice. Some people use no-follow backlinks to keep track of the links that they have shared. If you want to rank higher, you should have a mix of no-follow and do-follow backlinks. You can make a no-follow backlink by inserting the no-follow attribute. The no-follow attribute that you have added to the link prevents search engine spiders from crawling the linked site.Backlinks are powerful; they have far-reaching and long-lasting effects on your SEO success. Backlinks may be from other websites. For example, when you click on a link in a text to a certain page, a local search engine will consider your click to be a connection from that website to the hyperlinked page. If that link directs visitors to a page about dogs, then a local search engine will assign your click a lower ranking on that page and not include it in the search engine’s list of links to pages about dogs. On the other hand, if that same link directs visitors to a page about flowers, a local search engine will also consider your click to be a connection from that website to the hyperlinked page, but it will also include your click in the list of links to pages about flowers. So, backlinks are important as they help to rank a website in a search engine’s organic (unpaid) search results.A link is any such relationship between pages. If you’ve just written a great article on affiliate marketing, and another article describes a specific affiliate marking program for a particular country, then the backlink between them will be that specific link. This isn’t to say that all backlinks are equal. Links that appear on the pages that you want to get links from are worth more than links that appear on pages that you have little or no interest in. However, links on the same web page from pages about the same subject will be worth much more than links that appear from other pages about the same subject.Now that you know what backlinks are, what they are not, and what SEO is, it’s time to get to work. Look over your content once again, and start crafting a backlink strategy. Create sub-domains, comment on your competitors’ websites, use banner ads and the likes, and link to your content whenever possible. While you should aim to make as much content as possible accessible on other websites, remember that the big websites are more likely to use multiple backlinks to increase their ranking. If a big website is linking to you, then it’s a good sign. This will give you the best chance of climbing the ranks in the long run, so go get them.
