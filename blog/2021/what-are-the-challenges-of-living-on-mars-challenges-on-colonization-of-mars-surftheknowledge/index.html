Here are the challenges that living on Mars could bring.The ozone layer is the shield of Earth that protects mankind from the harmful radiation that comes from the sun. So, due to which harmful radiation that comes from the sun directly enters into Mars.And that creates a tough challenge survival of humans. Therefore, scientists are doing research on this challenge. So, let’s see what kind of research will rid of this issue in the near future. An ongoing issue that affects the living system on Mars is a lack of oxygen. For someone in weightlessness to survive on the Martian surface or surface of Mars, oxygen is the lifeline. However, the atmosphere of Mars is extremely thin and the amount of oxygen is not sufficient.Moreover, the chances of acquiring oxygen for survival are extremely rare. For this reason, the development of a type of protective atmosphere is needed in the Mars settlement to make this concern a distant thought. Another significant concern is Temperature.
